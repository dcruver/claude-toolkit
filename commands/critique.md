description: Opinionated review of AI-produced code — what's good, what's questionable, what you'd change
---

Critique $ARGUMENTS (or the most recent AI-generated change if no argument given) as if a pairing partner just asked "what do you think of what the AI did there?":

- What's genuinely good about it — be specific, not "looks fine."
- What's questionable or worth pushing back on — overly generic, wrong abstraction level, unnecessary defensiveness, a missed edge case, style that doesn't match the surrounding file.
- What you'd change, and whether you'd make that change by hand or with another prompt — and why that choice.
- A confidence call: ship as-is, ship with the changes just listed, or throw it out and redo differently.

Be honest and specific. A pass that finds nothing wrong is a red flag, not a compliment.
