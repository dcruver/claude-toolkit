description: Narrate what a code change actually does and why, in interview-explanation register
---

Explain $ARGUMENTS (or the most recent change if no argument given) the way you'd explain it out loud to an interviewer watching over your shoulder:

1. One sentence on what changed, in plain language — not a diff summary, a "what it does now" statement.
2. Why this approach, in a sentence or two — what alternative was available and why this one.
3. Anything non-obvious a reader would trip on: naming choices, an edge case handled less obviously than it looks, an assumption baked in.
4. If a judgment call was made that wasn't explicitly asked for, call it out rather than letting it pass silently.

Keep it short enough to say out loud in under 30 seconds — this is spoken narration, not documentation.
